Project policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · Contact: ylobodzynskyi@safeyourlove.com

This policy explains how Threadsignal processes information when you use its Threads post analysis prototype or this information site. Threadsignal is operated by the project developer at the contact address above.

Information we process

  • Draft content: post text and an optional topic you enter for analysis.
  • Comparison data: text, public post URLs, usernames, dates, and available view or interaction counts retrieved through a configured Threads API connection or imported by the operator.
  • Technical data: GitHub Pages and other hosting providers may process standard request information such as IP address and browser details to deliver this site.

The current prototype does not offer end-user accounts or ask for your Threads password. Its Meta access token and TypeSafe API key are held on the application server, not in the browser.

How we use information

We use draft text to produce structured Jev judgments, find relevant comparison posts, and show writing suggestions. We use comparison data to identify strong examples and calculate experimental estimates. We do not use draft text to publish a Threads post.

Service providers and sharing

Draft text and short excerpts of comparison posts are sent to TypeSafe AI for analysis. If a Threads connection is enabled, search requests are sent to Meta's Threads API. This policy site is hosted by GitHub Pages. The prototype interface loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive technical request data. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. These providers may process data under their own policies.

Storage and retention

Drafts are processed for the response and are not written to the application's post database. Imported or fetched comparison posts are stored in the local data/posts.json file until removed by the operator. A local installation remains under the control of the person running it. TypeSafe and hosting providers may retain information under their own policies; see the links above.

Your choices and deletion

You can avoid including personal or sensitive information in a draft. To request deletion of information held by the project operator, follow the data deletion instructions or email ylobodzynskyi@safeyourlove.com. We may ask for enough information to identify the relevant record, but never for your password.

International processing and children

TypeSafe and hosting providers may process data outside your country. Threadsignal is not directed to children under 18.

Changes

We will update this page when the application or its data practices change and revise the effective date above. Contact us at the address above with privacy questions.
